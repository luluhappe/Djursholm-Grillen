From Sibylla to Djursholmsgrillen

In 2026, longtime friends Jon Sindénius and Mikael Söderlindh opened Djursholmsgrillen in the former Sibylla spot on Djursholm’s town square. They wanted a neighborhood grill where students could stop by after school and families could share a casual meal.

Their menu combines Swedish grill classics with Japanese-inspired flavors. Jon developed the menu and runs the grill day to day. Mikael leads the restaurant’s concept and design.
